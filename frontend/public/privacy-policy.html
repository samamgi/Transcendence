Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-07-28

1. Data We Collect

  • Account data: username, email address, encrypted password.
  • Profile data: display name and avatar image.
  • Game data: match scores, outcomes, and statistics.
  • Social data: friends, conversations, and message reactions.
  • Session data: technical cookies to keep you logged in securely.

2. Why We Use Your Data

  • To authenticate users and secure accounts.
  • To provide gameplay, matchmaking, and tournament features.
  • To support social features (friends, chat, notifications).
  • To compute profile statistics and match history.
  • To maintain service reliability and basic abuse prevention.

3. Data Storage and Retention

User and gameplay data are stored in a PostgreSQL database used by the application backend. Data is kept while the account remains active. If you delete your account, related personal data is removed from active records as part of the account deletion flow.

4. Security

Passwords are hashed before storage. Session cookies are HTTP-only. Access controls are enforced for authenticated routes and private resources. While reasonable technical safeguards are applied, no system can guarantee absolute security.

5. Your Rights

  • Access and update your profile data.
  • Delete your account from within application settings.
  • Request clarification about stored personal data.

6. Contact

For privacy-related requests, contact the project maintainer through the repository communication channel used for the ft_transcendence project.
